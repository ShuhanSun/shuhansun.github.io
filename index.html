Research Interests

AI Agent Safety

Detecting and preventing unsafe agent actions, especially when agents interact with tools, APIs, files, browsers, and external systems.

Prompt Injection & Adversarial Agents

Understanding how untrusted instructions propagate through agent workflows and designing defenses that remain effective under realistic attacks.

Constraint-Aware Decision Systems

Decision orchestration that optimizes utility while respecting safety constraints, policies, business rules, user preferences, and system limits.

Evaluation & Benchmarks

Building reproducible evaluations for agent reliability, policy compliance, attack resistance, and failure modes beyond conventional model accuracy.
